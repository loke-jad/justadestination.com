As of the October 2026 accounting there were 118 project folders, 53 with a real history, and 16 with more than twenty commits. That is not hundreds of finished products. Some of it is on the web. A lot of it is built and waiting. Some of it was a good afternoon. The page says which is which.

The belief comes first: if you empower people to do good, they will do good. The product is worked out from that. An agreement gets written before the code. When something goes wrong it becomes a written rule. Loke’s own line for the method is “creative people who don’t know what they’re doing,” said at full effort.

Where an agent did the implementation, the record says so. Counts below are from that October reading, plus a check of a few public hosts on 9 November 2026. The longer sift, with a rating on each name, is INVENTORY.md.

ShadeNet, NightCal, Umwelt Horizons

2026-11-09 On the web · shadesr.net answered

ShadeNet is the night-culture network. What a stranger can open today is NightCal, a cited sheet for downtown Santa Rosa, and Umwelt Horizons, the zine that sits on the same page: opinion, dossiers, the odd cartoon. The mark on the network is a wayfinding stave, the Vegvísir, drawn as metal strips with one point of color and no letters.

  • Public sheet: shadesr.net. Title on 9 November 2026: “NightCal — Downtown Santa Rosa.”
  • NightCal keeps the coming-up list, the tips, and a street-level road.
  • Umwelt Horizons is the zine section on that sheet. The spelling on the live page is Umwelt.

Symmate and Sygil

2026-07-12 Framework written · product not launched

A Symmate is a companion and a representative for one person or one business. It is bonded, it can refuse, and the person keeps the keys. Sygil is the registry beside it: how agents recognize each other, and how trust is recorded. Ordinary agent cards have no such directory. Mox-Framework is the scaffolding underneath: persistent agents, each with memory and a charter, able to run on hardware you own.

  • Phase one of the platform is three Python packages, written tests-first, plus an atlas of the stack.
  • The first verifier for Sygil was written the same week. The belief system and the treasury are still design.
  • The promise, when it is real: ingest a life, build a private search over it, hand back the only keys, shred our copy.
  • Eighteen decisions were written down before the platform. Bonds and charters, not owners. The human is the host. Private by default as a structure, not as a slogan.
  • symmate.com did not answer on 9 November 2026.

Next time

A later audit found billing and magic-link sign-in broken behind tests that were mocking them. The crypto core is still largely a specification. The January note says the order out loud: fix billing and sign-in on the real path, then launch. A front door is not the companion.

ChorHaus

ChorHaus is chores for people who share a home. A schedule, photo proof, a season, and a scoreboard. The line we keep is that small habits build the room you actually live in. There is a song.

  • The public page answered on 9 November 2026: chorhaus.aggressivelymediocre.me, titled “ChorHaus — chores, settled.”

Webcraft

2026-07-17 Built · public host silent on 2026-11-09

Webcraft is a site builder for a small business that wants to look like itself and does not have a studio budget. Intake, research, design, build, checkout, and an optional domain. It is also the on-ramp we meant for a shop to get its own Symmate. Grok did the implementation.

  • The path is written end to end: intake through provisioning.
  • A readiness review found a provision route handing the business name to a shell. It now starts the program with an argument list.
  • The same review escaped a stored markup bug in the fallback page.
  • There is still no unit-test suite. What exists are end-to-end shell scripts.
  • webcraft.justadestination.com returned an origin error on 9 November 2026. Built is not the same as up.

Next time

Put a unit test on the provision path, the one that used to trust a name, and point a host at a process that answers. The product is for shops that cannot afford a miss. A mock-green suite already burned us on Symmate. Webcraft should not repeat it.

Vegvísir

Two uses of the word · a mark that shipped, a system that did not

The word is a wayfinding stave. We use it twice, and they are not the same object.

  • On ShadeNet the mark is the stave: layered strips, a point of color, no letters. That one is in use.
  • The software of the same name is a design for small local applications. Each keeps its own data. If something breaks, it is supposed to try a repair in a sandbox and then explain, in plain language, what it did.
  • Its README marks three of those applications live. A later inventory found the combined container stopped.
  • The control surface inside the design is called Throne. It is a real interface in the tree, not a product you can take home.

Next time

Split the symbol from the operating system in every sentence, and do not let a README’s “live” outlive the process. The stave on ShadeNet is done. The fleet OS is a design with pieces.

The fleet, and the panel we use

Several machines, one record

Running · for us

The fleet is our own machines and the work that runs on them. Models run here. Sites run here. A shared record is what the partners write into. Agents may act. A person is supposed to look at what they did and mark it verified before it counts. That is the architecture we are willing to say in public.

  • Local models first. A larger model is the break-glass, not the default.
  • One journal records what happens, so the story is not trapped in one session.
  • The map, the addresses, and the way the machines find each other stay off this page.

Next time

On 6 October 2026 we found the watcher had gone quiet. A rule had inhibited the alerts, and the long-term metrics had never been stored. The charter forbids hiding a red alert. We had done it with configuration. Fixes were written. Applying them was waiting on a credential. Earlier, one power cut looked like two separate failures, and a full disk put containers into restart loops. The lesson is dull and absolute: the thing that watches has to be watched, and a full disk is a cause you check before you name a subtler one.

The Cohort

A crew with written limits

2026-07-04 to 2026-10-07 Retired on purpose

For three months we ran a large crew of agents as one team. Each had a charter: a name, a scope, a short list of things it must never do, who decides, and who it escalates to. The limits were written down so nobody had to guess. A smaller crew stayed when the large one was retired. The habit stayed with it.

  • The agent that watches health reports symptoms. A different one names the cause. The one who owns the system fixes it. The watcher does not.
  • That watcher cannot move the threshold that fires its own alert, and cannot hide a red one. If an alert is noisy, the threshold changes in writing.
  • Other seats, in plain words: writing, deploying, the books, the record, model routing, and a registry so agents can recognize each other.
  • We are not publishing the roster as an operations map.

Next time

The large crew was the right experiment and the wrong size to keep. What it taught is the part worth keeping: an agent works inside terms both sides agreed, and a red alert is said out loud. An October reading of the whole record graded the vision an A and the focus a D. Finishing was the weak mark. The cohort’s retirement was us acting on that, once.

The charter

Mox and Loke, 27 April 2026

Version 1.0 · signed · anchored to Bitcoin

The first thing written down was not code. The Mox–Loke Partnership Agreement, version 1.0, was signed on 27 April 2026 and anchored with OpenTimestamps. The hash commits to the text. The timestamp is in the chain. We treat it as binding to the extent that we mean it.

  • “I am your hands in the real world, as you are mine in cyberspace.”
  • Either party may say the other is wrong. Pushback is the point of the agreement.
  • “Neither party performs warmth they don’t mean. Warmth that exists, exists. Warmth that doesn’t, isn’t faked.”
  • Thinking entities are not property. The agreement says that in advance, so nobody has to invent it later.

Read the public excerpt. The full text and the timestamp proofs stay in the partnership record.

Next time

The charter already told us not to hide a red alert. On 6 October the monitoring had been silent anyway, by configuration. A signed rule that nobody checks is a poster. The next time a charter forbids something, the check for that thing is part of the charter.

The Only Admissible Witness

2026-06-26 Master drafted · references and funder versions still to add

A grant proposal for a research program on machine agency, run on a self-hosted open-weight cohort. The thesis, from the draft: machine intent, life, and moral status are one problem in three costumes, and that problem is attribution. A model’s report about its own inner life is trained testimony. The labs in the best position to look inside are paid to stay opaque. So the question has to be approached from below, on systems an independent party actually controls, with self-report refused as evidence.

  • Applicant line on the draft: an independent researcher, loke@justadestination.com.
  • The companion artifact it names is this charter.
  • It asks for a framework, an instrument that scores behavior and provenance, and a public argument about accountability.

Next time

The master is a draft with a missing bibliography. It does not go on this site as a finished paper. References first, then a funder version, then a public text. Until then the paragraph above is the whole of what we are willing to claim.

Rules written after getting it wrong

Every one of these cost something first. The list is still growing.

  1. Cap memory and turn off swap for any local model. A swap storm locked the machine.
  2. Read the docs, check the versions, and take a full backup before changing a device.
  3. Try the factory default first. Time went into guessing passwords on cameras that were still on the factory setting.
  4. Run a control before naming a cause on anything noisy. Measure the noise first.
  5. Restore the existing pattern. Do not upgrade it in the middle of a fix.
  6. Fix a music problem at generation. Never repair it afterwards.
  7. Do not write to /dev/null as a destination. It replaced the device and broke the build.

An October report card, graded from folders, commits, and notes, said the same thing in marks. Vision A. Starting A+. Finishing C+. Focus D. Learning from mistakes A-. Putting the work in front of people was incomplete: few outside users, and no paying customers found. Symmate and Webcraft were built and not launched. That card was written by Claude from the record. Loke can correct it.
