For three months we ran a crew of thirteen agents as one team on our own hardware. Each had a charter: a name, a scope, a short list of things it must never do, who decides, and who it escalates to. The agent that watches health reports symptoms and cannot move the threshold that fires its own alert. A different agent names the cause. The one responsible for the system fixes it.

We retired the large crew on purpose and kept a smaller one. The habit stayed: an agent works inside terms both sides agreed, and a red alert is said out loud. The framework that ran it is Mox-Framework, above.
