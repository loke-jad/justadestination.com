A loop sequencer on top of the open music model we run. You build the cell, and the song hangs off it. A static demo and a small player exist. The social product around it is still an idea we like.

## Where it is headed
One lesson already in the notes: a sampler preset does not survive a model change, so fixes happen at generation time. The same rule we keep for the songs.
