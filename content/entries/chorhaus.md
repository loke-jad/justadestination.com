ChorHaus is chores for people who share a home, turned into a friendly contest. A schedule, photo proof, a season, and a scoreboard. The idea we keep coming back to is that small habits build the room you actually live in. There is also a song.

## What it does today
- Households define their chores, check them off with photo proof, and watch a season play out on the leaderboard. The winner is whoever earns the most points for work that was defined and checked.
- Coach Jordan, an in-app companion, keeps the house talking and proposes fixes when a routine slips.
- Version two is one service: the public page and the arena behind it, Google sign-in, anti-cheat on the proof, and a subscription credit capped at what a person paid.

## Where it is headed
Version two has a short, written pre-deploy checklist left to close. Pooled cash pots are parked on purpose until we have had legal advice, because a skill contest should never drift into a lottery.
