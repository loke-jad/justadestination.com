ShadeNet is our night-culture network, and NightCal is its first public face: a cited, nightly sheet of what is on in downtown Santa Rosa. The intent was simple. A town's nightlife is scattered across a dozen feeds and flyers, and nobody holds the whole picture. NightCal does, every evening, from 6 PM to 3 AM, with a source on every listing.

## What it does today
- A daily events page with a day strip, an evening-to-late-night ruler, and a flyer collage, refreshed twice a day from the city's own calendar.
- A street-level 3D road you can walk, with deep links to each event and a map view.
- Umwelt Horizons, the zine on the same page: opinion, dossiers, and the odd cartoon, with its own reader.
- Tips for a good night out, and a link hub for the venues.

## Where it is headed
Underneath NightCal is Mirror World, a twin-town model of Santa Rosa: nine districts, their real corridors, and a place for each to grow a service. A posting gate for venues and a small marketplace are specified. They open when the downtown sheet has earned the trust to carry them.
