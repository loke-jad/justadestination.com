A Symmate is a companion and a representative for one person or one business. It carries your context, speaks for you where you ask it to, and is bonded to you by a charter rather than held by anyone. Your data is encrypted under keys you hold. Sharing is your choice, and private by default is a structure, not a slogan. Sygil is the trust layer beside it: a registry so agents can recognize each other, and a record of how trust was earned.

## What exists today
- Mox-Framework, the open core underneath: persistent agents with a soul, a charter, memory, skills, and a mailbox, running on an open-weight model behind any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. It is extracted from the cohort that ran on our fleet.
- Phase one of the hosted platform: three Python packages written tests-first, an operator-blind database schema with no plaintext personal data, and an atlas of the whole stack.
- The first Sygil verifier, plus a preflight scanner with payments wired in end to end.
- Eighteen numbered decisions, written before the platform, about what a Symmate is and what it refuses to become. Bonds and charters, not property. The human is the host.
- A claims audit that polices the copy we write: we say "encrypted under keys you hold," never "unhackable."

## Where it is headed
Billing and sign-in on the real path first, then launch. The order is written down. The sealed-memory core moves from specification to shipped code slice by slice, and the hosted tier earns stronger privacy claims only when the hardware attestation that backs them is live.
