A game about making a game. You talk to a character called Pixel, your design document grows as you talk, you earn experience, and harder ideas unlock until you can build the thing yourself. It is part of our AggressivelyMediocre label, where learning by doing is the whole point.

## What it does today
- A dual-agent loop: Pixel chats, an invisible editor extracts the design document from the conversation in real time.
- Experience, levels, an inventory, and a plot-hole boss fight, with single sign-on and a gateway that can call our image, speech, and 3D tools.

## Where it is headed
It comes back online when it has a home that stays up. Until then it is a prototype we are fond of.
