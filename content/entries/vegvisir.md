The word is a wayfinding stave, and we use it twice. On ShadeNet it is the mark: layered metal strips, one point of color, no letters, and that one is in use. The software of the same name is a design for a self-healing swarm of small local applications. Each keeps its own data, talks to its siblings over a private network, and when something breaks it tries a repair in a sandbox and explains, in plain words, what it did.

## Where it is headed
The control surface, called Throne, and the first applications exist in the tree. The combined container is not running today. The design stands; the swarm follows.
